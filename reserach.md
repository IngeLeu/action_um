# Research

The ACTION Lab studies how dynamic brain networks support action, cognition, and adaptive behaviour.

## Action control and decision making

We investigate how beta and theta oscillations contribute to movement control, inhibitory control, and decision making.

## Brain stimulation

We use non-invasive brain stimulation to test the causal role of brain rhythms in cognition and behaviour.

## Neurochemistry and MRS

We study neurochemical mechanisms, including GABA, using magnetic resonance spectroscopy.

## Brain-body coupling

We examine how interactions between brain activity, bodily signals, and behaviour shape cognition.

## Clinical motivation

Our work is largely fundamental, but often starts from clinical questions related to Parkinson’s disease, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and other disorders involving dysfunctional brain networks.
